Ortis Letters Digital Edition

The prototype for the digital edition of the "Ultime Lettere di Jacopo Ortis" ("Last Letters of Jacopo Ortis") following Giovanni Gambarin's National Edition (1970).

The editorial history of The Last Letters of Jacopo Ortis runs parallel to the life of its author Ugo Foscolo. Tracing and comparing the different versions of the work helps us understand Foscolo’s editorial choices, which reflect the evolution of his personal experiences and circumstances.

The Editions

Bolognese Draft, 1798

The edition of a draft that was never published, and later modified by Angelo Sassoli.

Vera Storia di Due Amanti Infelici ossia Le Ultime Lettere di Jacopo Ortis, 1799

The first published edition of the work, not approved by Ugo Foscolo, with Angelo Sassoli's additions.

Goethe's Draft, Milan, 1801

A draft, the only known version of this edition that was never published but gifted to Goethe.

Ultime Lettere di Jacopo Ortis, Genio Tipografico, Milan, 1802

The Editio Princeps, the first published edition of the work formally approved by Ugo Foscolo.

Ultime Lettere di Jacopo Ortis, Orell and Füssli, Zurich, 1816

The second published edition of the work, published in Zurich but 'London' on the frontespice for censorship reasons.

Ultime Lettere di Jacopo Ortis, John Murray, London, 1817

The last published edition of the work, issued in London.
